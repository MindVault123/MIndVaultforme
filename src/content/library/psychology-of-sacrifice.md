## The Illusion of Nobility
We often think sacrifice is inherently good. *When we bleed for others without setting boundaries, we aren't being noble; we are being self-destructive.*

## The Sunk Cost Fallacy in Relationships
The more you give, the harder it is to leave. This is why toxic relationships survive. *You aren't staying because of love; you're staying because of your investment.*
