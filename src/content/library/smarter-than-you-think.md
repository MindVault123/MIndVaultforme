## The Curse of Overthinking
Anxiety and intelligence are highly correlated. *Your inability to sleep might just be your brain refusing to shut down its problem-solving engine.*

## Adaptability Over Knowledge
Real intelligence isn't about what you know; it's about how fast you can unlearn what's wrong. *The smartest people are the first to admit they were incorrect.*
